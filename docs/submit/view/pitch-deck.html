The problem

Why this exists

AI agents can spend. Soft limits are not enough.

They talk around prompts

If permission lives only in chat, a clever prompt can walk past it.

Money needs a hard box

Teams need a temporary mandate with purpose, budget, merchants, and an ending.

Someone else must read it

When something goes wrong, a second person should reconstruct the story from a trail.

Not a chatbot. Not a wallet clone. 02 / 10
CLAMP
What it is

Product

A delegation control layer for agents that spend.

The agent may act alone only inside a temporary mandate you create. Outside that box, money does not move.

Inside every mandate

  • purpose
  • budget including fees
  • allowed merchants
  • expiry
  • revocable anytime
Hard box. Clear story. 03 / 10
