Likely questions

Is this just a wallet limit UI?

No. It’s a delegation control layer. Mandate plus deterministic gate plus a reconstructable audit trail. Kiln is for parse and explain only.

Why not let the model decide allow or deny?

That burns tokens and can be socially engineered. We parse once, decide in code, explain once.

What is on chain?

Mandate commits and decision receipts on Base Sepolia through ClampAudit. Hashes and outcomes. Testnet is labeled.

What if the case is fuzzy?

Needs human. Payment stays stopped until a person decides.

Can someone fake a receipt?

Writers are access controlled. Final decisions are backed by real Base Sepolia tx hashes in FastAPI.

Is this production custody?

No. Control and records for Challenge B. Honest about testnet and residual risk. Not claiming unhackable.

Who built what?

Song: Kiln, gate, FastAPI, metering, audit payloads. Henry: product UI, sessions, ClampAudit, pitch and demo package.
